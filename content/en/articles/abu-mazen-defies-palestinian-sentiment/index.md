### by Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem

Without the slightest concern, hesitation, or respect — with utter mockery, derision, and contempt for the Palestinian people — Abu Mazen (Mahmoud Abbas), president of the self-rule authority in Ramallah, telephones the filthy Zionist criminal who has killed thousands of Arabs — Palestinians, Lebanese, Jordanians, Egyptians, and Syrians — Ehud Olmert, to congratulate him on his acquittal from the corruption charges brought against him in the Zionist courts.

This hideous killer, this bloody butcher who thrives on the suffering of the people of Palestine, on the blood of their martyrs, the groans of their children, and the sighs of their women, receives a friendly phone call from a man who claims to represent the people of Palestine.

If only Abu Mazen would explain to the people the compelling reasons behind this congratulatory call. With it, he is telling the people of Palestine, at home and abroad: "Your suffering, you fools, sits deep in Olmert's belly, and if you have any life left at all, Olmert is one of the heroes who provided it to you."

This Olmert, Mr. President of the Self-Rule Authority, is the murderer of my brother
