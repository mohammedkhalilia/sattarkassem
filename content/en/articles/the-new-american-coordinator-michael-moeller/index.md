### by Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem

The new American security coordinator between Palestinians and the Zionists is Michael Moeller. Moeller previously held the position of director of strategy, planning, and policy command at U.S. Central Command, based at MacDill Air Force Base in the American state of Florida. He is a distinguished military commander, possessing diverse skills in strategy, planning, and coordinating dual and overlapping military operations, and has received a large number of military decorations for his accomplishments. He holds the rank of Major General, exactly the rank held by some commanders of the Palestinian security apparatuses.

I could not find his date of birth, but I infer from the stages of his education that he is in his mid-fifties, or slightly less — perhaps 52 or 53. He holds a bachelor's degree in geography from the military academy in Colorado Springs, Colorado, in 1980; a military command certificate from Maxwell Air Force Base in Alabama in 1984; a master's degree in aeronautical science from Embry-Riddle University in Daytona Beach, Florida, in 1984; he studied at the Air Command and Staff College at Maxwell in Alabama in 1993; a master's in airpower art and science at Maxwell in Alabama in 1994; a joint forces staff command course at Norfolk, Virginia, in 1996; a National Defense Fellowship from the Center for Strategic and International Studies in Washington in 1999; and a national security leadership course from Syracuse University, New York, in 2005.

Moeller has been entrusted with many tasks, moving through a number of positions. Accordingly, he has received many decorations and medals, including: the Defense Superior Service Medal; the Legion of Merit with Oak Leaf Cluster (a group known for its symbolic military importance in the United States); Distinguished Command Pilot wings; the Bronze Star Medal with Oak Leaf Cluster; the Defense Meritorious Service Medal; the Meritorious Service Medal with three Oak Leaf Clusters; and so on.

Moeller will come to Palestine after Congress confirms his appointment. Naturally, we do not welcome him in the slightest — indeed, we reject his desecration of our sacred homeland's soil. Moeller is one of the demons the United States dispatches, one after another, in rotation or in tandem, to our Arab countries, Palestine especially, in order to entrench its dominance and secure the subservience of Arabs to it and to the Zionists.

Moeller will come here primarily to oversee the security of the Zionist Jews from the Palestinians, and to follow up on the work of pursuing the Palestinian rifle, indeed the free Palestinian bullet, and pursuing resistance fighters, or anyone suspected of intending to resist Zionism in order to obtain the fixed national rights of the Palestinian people. Following in his predecessors' footsteps, Moeller will use Palestinians to arrest Palestinians, kill them, expel them from their jobs, and pursue them in their means and ways of making a living. He will come to commit crimes, to set Palestinian against Palestinian brother, to ensure the continuation of Palestinian schism and infighting, and the spread of animosity and hatred among Palestinians, so that no Palestinian cause remains. Following the pattern of his predecessors, this American devil will deceive Palestinians into believing he works in the Palestinians' interest, toward achieving their national project and establishing the Palestinian state. He will tell them the resistance fighters are agents of Safavid Shiite Iran, working to dominate the Arabs, and that killing Palestinian resistance "terrorists" serves the Palestinian national project, because they are agents of Iran and Hezbollah. He will convince them that these fighters do not actually want resistance, but rather work to sabotage the national project, and to subjugate Sunnis to Shiites in exchange for money and pleasures. He will send many of them to Jordan to receive military training suited to resisting the resistance. And we remember what Dayton did in Qalqilya.

Moeller will tell the Palestinians who will work with him that the only legitimate rifle is the Israeli-licensed rifle in the hands of the official Authority, while the secret rifle aimed at resisting Israel is a rifle of sabotage and terrorism. He will constantly remind them of the good intentions of the United States, and will give them statistics on the money America lavishes on the Palestinian security apparatuses, which it spends through its institutions operating in the West Bank.
