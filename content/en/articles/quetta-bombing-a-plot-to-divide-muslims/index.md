### by Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem

The Pakistani Quetta bombing clearly reveals the hidden hands working with malice to sow division among Muslims in order to weaken them and keep them under control. The explosion did not come by chance, nor as a result of friction between Shia and Sunni, but rather came about through prior planning to stoke the fire of sectarian strife among the Islamic schools of thought.

America and Israel are the ones with an interest in this explosion, aiming to divert Muslims' attention away from the resistance escalating in Iraq against the American occupation. America finds itself in a difficult predicament in Iraq, which does not sit well with Israel, which wishes to see all Muslims humbled and subdued. These two satanic states try to manufacture events on the Islamic scene that lead to internal Islamic fighting or discord, so that resistance to the occupation retreats in favor of self-assertion against internal rivals. Now is the American-Israeli season for committing various crimes in order to facilitate the humiliation of the people of Iraq and the continuation of the occupation of Palestine.

It is the duty of Muslim scholars of every school, party, and current to stand as an impenetrable wall against these American-Israeli crimes, and to work to extinguish every fire of sedition kindled by the enemies of the nation. It is the duty of these scholars to break out of their cocoon and become mujahideen in a time that demands jihad from every Muslim man and woman.

Sinful is he who does not fight the division of the Muslims
