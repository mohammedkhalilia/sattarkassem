### by Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem

The American Central Intelligence Agency has opened training centers for Palestinians, aimed at suppressing the Palestinian people as it defends itself and resists in order to reclaim its rights. Officially, America funds these training centers, but we know America does not fund anything for the sake of God, nor out of love for the Palestinian people, nor in support of Palestine's poor. America funds in order to serve its own purposes and Israel's goals. Right now, centers for training Palestinians are active in Jordan, under the Hashemite regime, which does not hesitate to commit any vile act capable of sapping the nation's strength and turning it into a mount for its enemies.

Intelligence circles are careful to select Palestinians with particular qualifications for training and returning them to Palestine. The candidate for training is required to be marked by naivety, foolishness, and failure at school and in life generally, and to be a young man in the prime of his youth as much as possible, unless his low qualifications excuse an age above twenty-five. He must also have no patriotic past pulling him toward the homeland, or making him think of a national act someday, and must be eager for cheap money steeped in humiliation and disgrace. He is also required not to come from a respectable, homeland-loving family, or one endowed with a patriotic or moral conscience the candidate might have absorbed. Beyond this, the candidate for training must not know God, glory and exaltation be to Him, and preferably should be among those who curse the prophets, peace be upon them, and offend against the Divine Self. Naturally, it is preferable that he have a poor reputation and poor conduct.

In short, the candidate is required to be, as the Iraqis would say, a lowlife; as the Egyptians would say, a leech; or as the people of Greater Syria would say, a scoundrel. He must be fallen and effeminate, easy to train into further falling and effeminacy.

What do they do with him in Jordan? They make him understand that he is doing great national work for the sake of establishing the independent Palestinian state with holy Jerusalem as its capital, and explain to him that there are people in Palestine who do not want a state established, because their interests are tied to the nation's enemies, such as Shiite Iran, which wants to dominate the Sunnis, and that there are those who seduce young people in Palestine, promising them paradise if they are martyred fighting Israel, and that there is a rabble who believe what these religious and national one-upmanship artists tell them. They tell them Israel is strong, cannot be defeated, that America supports it with all its might, and that it is a state that respects human beings and wants to live in peace and love with the Palestinians. They portray Israel as a dove of peace, with the problem lying only with Palestinian extremists and terrorists. They spare no effort in falsifying many matters, to instill in them the conviction that they are doing work that serves the homeland and its citizens — so that when they return to Palestine, they carry a sword to cut off heads.

I say to these young men now training in Jordan, funded by American intelligence, and to their fathers and mothers:

You are being led astray, because you are serving the enemies of God, the nation, the people, and the homeland. You are enlisting yourselves in the service of the invading Zionists who displaced the people and seized the homeland, and in the service of Israel's and America's agents among Palestinians, Jordanians, and other Arabs. Tomorrow, you will open fire on the sons of Palestine, its mujahideen and fighters, and at the very least you will confront your own protesting people, demanding their rights, with batons and clubs, and you will arrest them and throw them into prisons. It is true that some of you need the scraps of money they pay you, but remember: a nation dies rather than feed from its own breasts. We must live with honor and die with honor.

Some of you are deceived by the Palestinian government currently in place in Ramallah. How could you not be, when it is the very government the Americans give Arab money to, so it can pay salaries, entitlements, and the like? They deceive you with some of the money they give from the Arab nation's own account, which pays most of the funds the donor states provide. This money is impure, even though I am personally convinced that vast segments of the Palestinian people need money to go on with life. It is impure money, steeped in humiliation, disgrace, and disappointment, because it comes in exchange for silence about your rights and your holy sites. The government currently in place is not a Palestinian government at all — it is a government serving American policy and Israeli goals, and our evidence for this is abundant. I remind you here that the global Masonic movement believes in the necessity of the Jews' return to Palestine, and believes that peace and love in the world cannot be achieved except through the building of Solomon's Temple.

Some of you will say this writer is a university professor drawing a salary, unconcerned whether others go hungry. I only remind you that I am the author of a national project for self-sufficiency, which I have put forward again and again, so that we might rely on ourselves and spare ourselves humiliation and abasement — but regrettably, the Palestinian factions show no interest, and vast segments of the people are not attentive to the danger of depending on others. Great peoples never lack solutions, because they are living, creative peoples who refuse to part with honor and pride, and it is only abject nations that accept mortgaging their daily bread to their enemy.

You who are undergoing this training: you would achieve a great deed if you turned your backs on American and Arab intelligence, and returned with your heads held high, refusing to become security agents for the Zionists and the Zionists' helpers. The hardship of a meager life is far easier to bear than being pointed at with the finger of collaboration and betrayal.
