Dr. Abdul-Sattar Denies Having Threatened Anyone

Published today at 12:14

Bethlehem - Ma'an - Lecturer and thinker Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem today, Saturday, denied what was reported on the Feras Press website that he had threatened to hold accountable everyone who had burned his car and everyone who had brought him before the courts and had him arrested.

Dr. Kassem denied, in a phone call with Ma'an News Agency, that he had threatened anyone, and stated that what was published on that website is baseless.
