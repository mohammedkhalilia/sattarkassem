### by Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem

Our Arab Muslim Iraqi people are writing the loftiest verses of sacrifice and self-giving in the face of the most arrogant military machine in the world. Our mujahideen and fighters embrace death in martyrdom for the sake of a life of dignity, honor, and pride. They are not intimidated by tanks or planes, nor deterred by massacres, the killing of children, and the demolition of homes. They are a people who have believed in their Lord and in jihad in defense of Iraq's soil and of the entire nation. They are writing the most magnificent examples, making history and inscribing within it its most radiant pages.

Men and women who have dedicated themselves as knights to restore that glory which has faded and that honor which our forefathers built for us.

The age of the cowardly Arab has come to an end. In Palestine, in Lebanon, in Iraq, souls and hearts are uniting in one man's faith, casting off the garment of humiliation and setting out with steadfastness and pride toward building a future for the generations. The Muslim Arab has resolved to seize his freedom, and America will find nothing from him but the utmost toughness and might.

Let everyone stand with our Arab Muslim people in Iraq: in Fallujah, Ramadi, Mosul, Baqubah, Najaf, Karbala, Basra, Baghdad, Nasiriyah, Amara, Samawah, Tikrit, Ana, Rawa, al-Musayyib, Diyala, Kirkuk, Sulaymaniyah, and all its cities and villages. This is the decisive battle: either America grows even more arrogant and extends its aggression, along with that of the Zionist entity, to other Arab lands, or it suffers a resounding defeat that heralds the decline of its crimes and those of the hateful Zionist entity.
