### by Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem

The alarm bell of Freemasonry in Palestine has been ringing loudly for some time, its peals growing more forceful and vigorous by the day, Masonic influence growing day after day, and the numbers of Freemasons on the rise. Masonic activities are under way in the West Bank, and there are attempts to draw in influential and impactful figures in society, which constitutes a new and dangerous threat to the Palestinian people and the Palestinian homeland. Freemasonry is an extremely dangerous organization, far more dangerous than global Zionism, working diligently and silently, achieving great accomplishments in the service of the rabbis of the Jews, who seek control over peoples, or, at the very least, seek to pollute cultures and trivialize the thinking of young men and women, reducing them to mere consumers dazzled by the pleasures of Western civilization.

The dangers of Freemasonry come from the following:

First: Freemasonry is a historic organization that arose in Babylon, its purpose being to return to the Holy Land and rebuild Solomon's Temple, because it is a symbol of love and peace. This is the origin of the word "Masonry," meaning the builders' movement — those who work to build the Temple. The movement was renewed in a new form in the modern era, its ultimate goal being the establishment of Solomon's Temple in the city of Jerusalem, on the premise that peace in the world cannot come about except through building the Temple.

Second: The Masonic movement is led and directed by Jews, but its membership includes people of various religions and nationalities. These leaders saw it as important to recruit others to serve their ambitions and goals, but in a smooth, non-alienating, and sometimes even attractive manner. Of course, membership is not open to everyone — its leaders focus on people of decision-making power, influence, and thought, such as rulers, ministers, and men of money and thought, because they want to seize control over decision-making in states, and to be assured that states where Freemasonry has spread do not make decisions that fail to serve Jews or that oppose their policies. Membership is restricted to men, excluding women.

Third: Freemasons approach individuals who constitute points of attraction under the banner of charitable and social work that brings good to the public, particularly to those in need. At first they appear as angels striving for the good of humanity, serving orphans and widows, and pushing the country forward across various fields. For this reason, targets initially believe they will become elements of construction and revival for their homelands and peoples. Here I recall one of my students at the University of Jordan, when I lectured on the dangers of Freemasonry, saying that Freemasonry is not like that at all and does good, because "my father is a Mason."

Fourth: Freemasonry costs whoever wants to join nothing, because it covers everything itself. Indeed, Freemasonry initially offers assistance to its members to improve their financial standing, which raises their degree of influence in society. Freemasonry provides financial and moral support, stirring envy among those not yet members and indirectly encouraging them to seek out someone to recruit them.

Fifth: The one joining Freemasonry is entangled from the very first day of his acceptance as a member — that is, on his initiation day. Freemasonry initiates its members, and has special rites for this. According to what the head of the Alexandria lodge, who reached the 33rd degree, the highest degree in the movement, has written, the person being initiated is stripped, a rope (halter) tied around his neck, made to crawl on all fours, and led to the lodge master, dragged like a donkey, so that the master can wave a sword over his neck, telling him he is accepted into the movement, and that his neck will be cut in just this way if he ever discloses its secrets. Some add that the initiated person is subjected to sodomy and photographed, so that he will never in the future be strong enough to leave the movement or disclose its secrets. I do not know whether sodomy is still practiced now among Arab Freemasons, particularly since the movement has lifted some of the secrecy around its activities in these countries, opening the field to a wide influx of people seeking positions and influence.

Sixth: A Freemason, upon entering the movement, sees three books — the Qur'an, the Gospel, and the Torah — as a symbol of the unity of religions. The Qur'an disappears once the person reaches the eighth degree, the Gospel disappears at the twenty-fifth degree, and the Torah remains — the very Torah now in the hands of the Jews. This is proof that the ultimate goal is to serve the goals of Jewish leaders.

Seventh: Freemasonry uses entanglement in its work, particularly moral entanglement. Entanglement begins on the initiation day with blackening the face, then moves on to entanglement through women or embezzlement of funds, and the like. This is a method also used by a number of Arab leaders to keep their own aides in check. Entanglement turns targeted leaderships into tools serving the Masonic movement, and through this, Freemasonry is assured that Zionist and Israeli goals will remain the guiding beacon for these leaders across the whole of their policies. This certainly explains the insistence of many Arab leaders on remaining within the circle of Israeli interests. An Arab leader is allowed to occasionally make statements against Israel and express his displeasure, in order to retain some credibility with his own people — but he is not allowed to go so far as to actually harm Israel.

**They Tried to Recruit Me**

Someone knocked on the door of my office at the University of Jordan in 1979, and I let him in. He greeted me warmly, with kisses and embraces, and then introduced himself, saying he was a doctor who had stopped practicing medicine because trade was more profitable. He was from a family in northern Jordan, and told me we were related by kinship, and indeed he was telling the truth, since one of my relatives was married to a relative of his, or the reverse — I no longer remember which. He then began showering me with praise until my head swelled from the sheer weight of the compliments and reverence. Then he said: "We discussed your name at the lodge, and we would be honored to have you as a member with us. We have His Majesty with us, and His Highness the Emir," and he listed for me a number of my own relatives whom I knew to be Freemasons. I threw him out of my office on the spot.

A short while later, I saw him sitting with one of my students in my Principles of Politics course, and my blood boiled, because I knew Freemasonry lures in women in order to entangle them. I hesitated to speak with the student, particularly since I was unmarried and eyes were watching me. In the end I mustered the courage to ask her about him, and she said he loved her and she loved him, and that they were going to marry. I did not dare continue the conversation. But I went back to her a second time, determined to tell her the truth. I spoke with her and asked her not to reveal what I was about to say. I told her that her beloved was a Freemason, that he would marry her tomorrow for a month or two, then throw her into the street for the dogs of Freemasonry to tear apart. As though I had asked her to keep my words secret — she told him.

One day, as I was approaching the gate of the University of Jordan in my car, I saw him standing there, gesturing at me to stop. I stopped. He thrust his head toward me angrily and asked what I had told the girl. I said to myself: "Man up, boy — this is the moment for courage." I flung the door open angrily and attacked him with shouting, saying: "You dogs, you trespass against our daughters, you scoundrels..." I saw him flustered as I shouted, and he pushed me back into the car, saying: "Let go of me — damn the day I ever laid eyes on you." Of course, I shouted loudly to create a scandal for him, and he backed off, preferring peace instead. And of course, I had already learned by that point that he worked for a security apparatus, his mission being to recruit University of Jordan professors into the Masonic movement, and he had certainly praised and lauded everyone he had met and made his offer to.

The student married him, stayed with him for two months, and then turned into someone I would rather not describe — God protect us from the accursed devil.

**Positions for Freemasons**

There are Arab states where Freemasonry has spread, such as Jordan, Tunisia, Egypt, today's Iraq, Morocco, Algeria, the Gulf states, and parts of Lebanon and the West Bank, and Masonic lodges exist in these countries, except for the Gulf states. Masonic meetings are now held openly in Arab countries, and Gulf Freemasons attend meetings in other countries such as Jordan, Lebanon, and Israel.

The highest, most fundamental positions in the Arab Masonic states go exclusively to Freemasons. A non-Mason cannot rise to a high position, because that would contradict the idea of Masonic dominance over the state and would endanger Israel's interests. So if you, dear reader, are interested in obtaining a position in this era marked by decline and loss, all you need to do is become a Freemason. Once you become a Mason, the path before you toward leadership will open up. They will make you a leader, and will offer you every kind of support to improve your image before your own people, and to keep suspicion away from you.

If you become a Mason, you will find yourself in the media without effort or exertion, and you will descend upon your people directly, as though you were born a bold, professional, and just leader. You are certainly hollow, with meager experience and scant knowledge, perhaps even degenerate morals — but they will turn you into something significant, and will achieve accomplishments for you that make you a hero. In exchange, however, you must sell your people and your homeland, and you will likely end up trafficking in your own wife. Do not be shocked if I tell you that you will attend parties that end in wife-swapping. You will enter the hall and place your house keys in a bowl, and when you leave, you will reach out and take a key at random, and then you will be assured that your wife will sleep with a rat like yourself, and you will sleep with a weasel like your own wife. So do you still want to be a Freemason?

Do you not notice how leaders suddenly appear in your country? There are those who achieve things across various fields, those who struggle and sacrifice, those who raise the banners of the homeland — yet they get no share of it. Suddenly, someone you have never heard of appears in the media, and within a few short weeks becomes a leader crowds gather around, chanting his name. Leaders do not fall from the sky — they sprout from the people; only agents have leadership come to them from places they cannot even trace.

**Freemasonry Is More Dangerous Than Zionism**

The star of Zionism has largely faded since the 1950s. It is true the Zionist organization still exists, but the tireless, diligent work now belongs to Freemasonry. Freemasonry has recruited leaders in East and West, in Arab and Muslim countries alike, and Israel has become confident that its own voice is the one that rises highest, and that its own interests are the ones that will win care and attention. Do you not see how the world erupts if someone spits on a Jewish grave in Japan, while it cares nothing for hundreds killed in explosions here and there? The reason is that a Jewish grave has "professional mourners," while the children of the poor have no one to weep for them. There are leaders recruited into the Masonic movement, and they do not stray from the path drawn out for them.

Why did they expose President Clinton? I do not know, but Monica Lewinsky's keeping her stained dress for several years means only one thing: that Clinton did something deserving of punishment. And indeed, they punished him and humiliated him. And if Clinton was subjected to scandal and humiliation, imagine the state of the Arab leaders who bray like donkeys the moment a woman's beauty catches their eye. These men broker away the Arab homeland, whose area exceeds 14 million square kilometers, not merely Palestine, whose area is 27,000 square kilometers. Does this solve the riddle of why Arab summits accomplish nothing useful? And why the Arabs continue making concessions in service of what is called peace? The matter is simple: these are rulers who control nothing of their own affairs, having sold themselves.

**Palestine, a Masonic Target**

There were Freemasons in Palestine before 1967, but they emigrated and were displaced because of the state of instability and out of fear of the people. But Freemasonry never truly stopped, continuing to operate in one form or another through forums such as Rotary and the Lions Club. Palestinian Freemasons revived themselves in Jordan, and some of them infiltrated back into Palestine, raising banners of patriotism as cover for concessions. Now, however, the matter appears more dangerous, because leaders of the Masonic movement are moving through the West Bank, working to recruit members of influence, impact, and decision-making power. And there are those in the very front ranks who, according to what has reached my ears, attend these meetings and take part in the recruitment process.

If I am telling the truth, you will find striking service projects under way in Palestine, such as the road network now being paved, and you will hear many national slogans — but you will find Israel persisting in its traditional policies of confiscating land, building settlements, Judaizing Jerusalem, and cutting the West Bank into pieces. If I am telling the truth, you will not see a real state under the current circumstances, but you will see people racing one another for positions and salaries, you will see the consumerist mentality growing ever more entrenched, and an accelerating shift toward national slogans without our ever seeing an actual homeland.

If I am telling the truth, you will find a focus on near-acrobatic media stunts, containing some genuine achievement, but ultimately aimed at diverting people's attention away from their fixed national rights. You will see Western financial and media support aimed at elevating certain people and increasing their popularity, but you will see more normalization, and more attacks by fallen intellectuals on people's awareness and their commitment to national rights, and you will find further decline among the youth and a drop in their concerns, and you will see a further decline in the level of education, at both the school and university levels.

I hope I am wrong.
