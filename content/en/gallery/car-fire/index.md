> **Renegade Fatah Elements and Abbas's Security Apparatuses Blow Up Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem's Car in Nablus**
>
> Renegade elements from the Fatah movement and Mahmoud Abbas's security apparatuses in the city of Nablus, in the occupied West Bank, detonated an explosive device in the private car of Dr. Abdul-Sattar Kassem — professor of political science at An-Najah University and a well-known political analyst — while he was inside his home, destroying the car and damaging the entrance of the house.
